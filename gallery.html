Places to visit

Explore landmarks, landscapes and heritage across the Lesotho highlands.

THE PLACES

Explore the destinations

Use these destinations as a starting point, then leave room to follow the view.

01 · WATERFALL

Maletsunyane Falls

A soaring waterfall near Semonkong.

See more

The single-drop falls plunge 192 metres. Take in the views, hike nearby, or ask local operators about guided activities and abseiling.

02 · HERITAGE

Thaba Bosiu

A landmark in Basotho history.

See more

King Moshoeshoe I established his mountain stronghold here in 1824. Visit the cultural village and learn about the formation of the Basotho nation.

03 · CULTURE

Kome Cave Dwellings

Sandstone homes with a remarkable story.

See more

See homes built into sandstone rock and learn about the families and traditions connected with this remarkable site.

04 · LAND & WATER

Katse Dam

A reservoir framed by highland scenery.

See more

The Katse Dam wall rises 185 metres. The reservoir and surrounding highlands make a memorable stop; check with LHDA for current visitor tours.

05 · WILDERNESS

Sehlabathebe National Park

Alpine scenery and ancient rock art.

See more

Part of the Maloti-Drakensberg transnational World Heritage property, the park is known for mountain scenery, rare species and San rock art.

06 · MOUNTAIN

Thabana Ntlenyana

A high-altitude summit for prepared trekkers.

See more

At 3,482 metres, this is the highest peak in Lesotho. Plan with experienced local support and prepare for changing high-altitude weather.
