MAKE IT YOUR OWN

Activities and services

Take the scenic route through the Mountain Kingdom with experiences led by local knowledge.

EXPERIENCES & SERVICES

Plan with local providers

Choose a guided outing, travel at your own pace, or build a trip around the places you most want to see. Arrange services directly with local operators and confirm details before you travel.

HERITAGE

Guided cultural visits

Visit historic and cultural sites with local guidance.

See more

Visit Thaba Bosiu, the mountain stronghold of King Moshoeshoe I, or explore Kome Cave Dwellings with a local guide. Ask in advance about opening times, guide availability and cultural protocols.

OUTDOORS

Pony trekking

Explore highland routes on a Basotho pony.

See more

Basotho pony treks offer a way to reach highland paths and rural villages. Options range from shorter rides to multi-day routes; confirm route length, equipment, accommodation and riding experience with the operator.

ADVENTURE

Hiking & waterfalls

Visit mountain trails and Maletsunyane Falls.

See more

Maletsunyane Falls near Semonkong drops 192 metres. Depending on local arrangements, visitors can enjoy viewpoints, hiking and abseiling; confirm current access and safety requirements before booking.

GETTING AROUND

Local transport

Plan travel between destinations.

See more

Mountain roads can be challenging and journey times vary. Arrange a suitable vehicle and driver for your route, and check road and weather conditions before departure.

STAYS

Lodges & local stays

Find accommodation near your route.

See more

Choose accommodation close to the places on your route. Booking ahead is especially helpful for remote areas; ask about meals, transport, facilities and local community experiences.

TRIP PLANNING

Custom itineraries

Build a trip around your interests.

See more

Plan realistic travel days between destinations. A local operator can help combine heritage visits, hiking, pony trekking and time to enjoy the scenery without rushing.
